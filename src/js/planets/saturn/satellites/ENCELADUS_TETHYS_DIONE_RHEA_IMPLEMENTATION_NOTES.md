# Enceladus, Tethys, Dione, and Rhea implementation

These four resolved Saturnian moons now use dedicated mapped surfaces instead of the shared procedural icy-moon branch.

## Enceladus

- Cassini cylindrical map with high-albedo ice colouring.
- Sparse crater population and global tectonic grooves.
- Four geometry-level south-polar tiger stripes with recessed floors and raised lips.
- Animated water-ice plume particles and a subtle south-polar scattering shell.
- No `PointLight` or other emitted illumination is attached to Enceladus.

## Tethys

- Cassini cylindrical map.
- Broad relaxed Odysseus basin with a shallow floor and rounded rim.
- A long broken canyon arc representing Ithaca Chasma.
- Dense but less saturated crater relief than Rhea.

## Dione

- Cassini mapped surface with slight leading/trailing colour differentiation.
- Cratered geometry and a restrained large southern basin.
- Braided trailing-hemisphere canyons with raised bright ice walls.

## Rhea

- Cassini/Voyager mapped surface.
- Densest crater population of these four models.
- Two broad impact basins and subtle fractured terrain.
- Slightly non-spherical geometry retained at a conservative scale.
