# Click-to-Explore Interaction — v11

## Free space

- The previous `+`, `−`, and percentage zoom controls were removed.
- Clicking a celestial body opens its normal focused inspection view.
- Clicking empty space recentres the camera toward that screen region and moves optically closer.
- Repeated empty-space clicks progressively magnify the selected region, making small planets and asteroid rocks easier to identify and select.
- Press `Escape` while no body is focused to return smoothly to the original free-flight framing.
- Vertical scrolling continues to control the Earth-to-deep-space journey.

## Focused body

- Drag to orbit around the selected body.
- Mouse-wheel scrolling or two-finger pinching performs the existing limited focus zoom.
- Closing the body card, pressing `Escape`, or clicking empty space restores the exact pre-focus journey position.

The click-to-explore offset and optical magnification do not rewrite the journey's distance-from-Earth measurement.
