# Celestial Perspective and Lighting v9

- The free-flight Solar System uses a cinematic Sun radius of 92 scene units.
- Planetary orbit spacing uses a larger shared scale so Mercury never intersects the enlarged Sun.
- Planet motion uses eccentric Kepler-style motion: mean anomaly advances uniformly and true anomaly is solved through Kepler's equation.
- Resolved asteroid motion now uses the same variable-speed orbital approach.
- During focused inspection of a planet, moon, or asteroid, the Sun is dynamically rescaled to reproduce its approximate real angular diameter from that body's heliocentric distance.
- The Sun therefore appears large from Mercury, about half a degree wide from Earth, and progressively smaller from the outer planets.
- A minimum optical halo keeps the distant Sun readable as a brilliant star.
- Asteroid albedo, point-particle visibility, and solar-light reach were increased while preserving a dark night side.
