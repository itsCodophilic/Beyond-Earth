# Celestial Scale Refinement v7

This build strengthens the visual diameter hierarchy while keeping the Solar System navigable.

- Earth is the visual baseline.
- Mercury and Mars remain smaller than Earth; Venus remains close to Earth size.
- Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune use a stronger compressed Earth-relative curve.
- The Sun is deliberately much more dominant without using the literal 109:1 diameter ratio.
- Asteroid sizes use a readable piecewise physical-diameter curve, preserving the order Ceres > Vesta/Pallas > Hygiea > Psyche > ordinary rocks.
- The asteroid belt restores brighter unresolved debris, larger solid rocks, enlarged invisible click proxies, and click-only screen-space selection for tiny GPU instances.
- During celestial focus, wheel scrolling or two-finger pinching changes only the local inspection distance. The underlying journey scroll and Earth-distance readout remain locked and restore exactly on exit.
