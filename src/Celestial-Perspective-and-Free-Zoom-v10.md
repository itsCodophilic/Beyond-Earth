# Celestial perspective and free zoom — v10

- Added independent optical zoom controls for every free-flight region.
- Free zoom changes camera FOV, not journey progress, so the Earth-distance readout remains stable.
- Focused bodies retain their existing bounded camera-distance zoom.
- Two-finger pinch works in both free flight and focus mode.
- Added a depth-tested distant-Sun star flare that strengthens as the solar disk becomes unresolved.
- The flare can be occulted by planets, while the solar point light continues to illuminate their Sun-facing hemisphere.
