# Beyond Earth: 100 new space events (proposal)

The site has 24 events today: formation, solar wind, CME, sungrazer, Mercury's sodium tail, meteor shower, solar eclipse, seasons, Earth's magnetic shield, lunar impact flash, Mars dust storm, the Jupiter and Saturn impacts, Io eruption, Saturn's white spot, ring spokes, Enceladus plumes, Uranus storms, Triton geysers, 'Oumuamua, Borisov, 3I/ATLAS, supernova and kilonova. None of those are repeated below.

**About this list**

- Everything here is natural, keeping the site's rule of no human-made objects. Spacecraft events like DART, Parker Solar Probe and the ISS are left out. A few events were *seen* by spacecraft, but the event itself is nature.
- Each line marks its type:
  - **Recorded:** happened, with a date.
  - **Recurring:** happens again and again.
  - **Ongoing:** happening all the time.
  - **Future:** predicted.
  - **Scale:** a size or distance shown, not something that happens.
  - **Theory:** the leading explanation, not observed directly.
- Dates were checked where they matter. Anything uncertain is marked as uncertain.

**Total: 110**, so we can cut down to the best 100.

**Easiest to build**, because the scene already has the bodies and similar effects:

- the size-and-scale set (1–12);
- the eclipses (23–25);
- Mars at opposition and retrograde (50–51);
- the ring-plane crossing (71);
- the Great Conjunction (96);
- the planet parade (97).

**Hardest:** most of section 11, which happens far outside the Solar System. They would play in the deep-sky backdrop, the way the supernova and kilonova do now.
