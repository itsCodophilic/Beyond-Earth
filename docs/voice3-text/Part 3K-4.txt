This is Leucus, one of the slowest-spinning objects known. One turn takes about four and a half days. Lucy will fly past it in twenty twenty-eight.

This is Orus, one of Jupiter's Trojans, and one of the worlds the Lucy spacecraft is on its way to visit.

This is Bienor, a stretched-out Centaur — perhaps two bodies, stuck together.

This is Asbolus, a Centaur with a bright, fresh patch on its surface — perhaps a recent crater.

This is Nessus, a Centaur, and one of the reddest objects in the Solar System.

This is Hylonome, a Centaur near the outer edge of the giant planets, close to where Neptune's pull takes over.

This is Euphrosyne, one of the largest asteroids — dark, with a moon of its own, on a steeply tilted orbit.

This is Koronis, which gives its name to a family of asteroids — the pieces of a world shattered long ago. Ida is one of them.

This is Eos, the parent of one of the largest families of asteroids.

This is Nysa, a bright asteroid that gives its name, with Polana, to a crowded complex of asteroid families.

This is Polana, a dark asteroid. Its family may be where Bennu and Ryugu came from.

This is Xewioso, a dwarf planet candidate beyond Neptune, and one of the darkest of these faraway worlds.

This is Achlys, a large world that moves in step with Neptune, just as Pluto does.

This is Uni, a large icy world beyond Neptune that seems to be lighter than water.

This is Aya, one of the largest worlds beyond Neptune.

This is Rumina, a world of water ice, out in the scattered disc beyond Neptune.

This is Ritona, a dwarf planet candidate beyond Neptune, named after a Gaulish goddess of river crossings.

This is Goibniu, a dwarf planet candidate beyond Neptune, named after the smith of the gods in Irish legend.

This is Manwë, a two-lobed world beyond Neptune, with a partner, Thorondor, going round it.

This is Teharonhiawako and its partner Sawiskera — twin brothers in Mohawk legend, and a pair of worlds circling each other in the cold, quiet Kuiper Belt.

This is Altjira, a world beyond Neptune that is probably not one but three.

This is Chiminigagua, a dwarf planet candidate out in the scattered disc, named after the creator of light in the legends of the Muisca people.
